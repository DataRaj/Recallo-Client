# Recallo identity study

## Positioning established before drawing

Recallo is a browser-based meeting workspace connecting a live conversation to its later record. The audience is people responsible for decisions and follow-up: small teams, consultants, interviewers and distributed collaborators. The identifier should remain useful if the product grows beyond video calls. Desired character: attentive, articulate, enduring; neither broadcast entertainment nor an autonomous AI persona.

Working promise: **Have the conversation. Keep the context.** The logo identifies the service; product copy explains feature availability.

## Methods inspected

Actual SKILL.md instructions were read, rather than only repository descriptions:

- [Kaan Kiziltug / logo-design](https://github.com/kaankiziltug/logo-design-skill/blob/main/skills/logo-design/SKILL.md): separate ideas before artwork, filled SVG outlines, optical corrections, one-color and small-size review. Its visual-techniques reference informed counter clearance and reverse treatment.
- [Fatih Aydost / brand-identity](https://github.com/fatihaydost/brand-identity-skill/blob/main/skills/brand-identity/SKILL.md): one mechanism ties symbol, lettering and palette together; measurements and taste judgments are distinguished. Cohesion and AI-default references also read.
- [Atypica / logo-design](https://github.com/atypica-ai/logo-design-skill/blob/main/skills/logo-design/SKILL.md): letter anatomy and name rhythm before vector construction. The double l and terminal o are useful features of Recallo.

These are methodological references, not evidence that a design has market-tested recognition. The requested five complete candidate kits take precedence over the repositories' usual three-concept/approval workflow. No external skill packages were installed.

## Category observations and similarity screen

Official sources inspected on November 12, 2026:

| Reference | Observation and consequence |
|---|---|
| [Slack media kit](https://slack.com/media-kit) | Multicolor interlocking hash. Avoid a radial arrangement of rounded colored pieces. |
| [Linear brand](https://linear.app/brand) | Diagonally segmented circular silhouette, disciplined monochrome use. Avoid striped discs; borrow only the discipline of standalone mono testing. |
| [Granola](https://www.granola.ai/) | Its official app icon is a hand-drawn spiral on chartreuse. Avoid coils, spiral recall metaphors and chartreuse as the identity's dominant ground. |
| [Otter](https://otter.ai/) | Audio/transcription-adjacent branding. Avoid audio bars, capsules and a sound-wave metaphor. |
| [Zoom](https://www.zoom.com/) | Familiar blue communication identity and rounded wordmark. Avoid a camera identifier and reliance on category blue. Media-center URL was inaccessible; homepage used instead. |
| [Notion](https://www.notion.com/) | Boxed initial and strong document associations. Avoid enclosing an initial in a drawn perspective box. Brand URL returned 401; homepage used instead. |
| [Raycast](https://www.raycast.com/) | Compact diagonal geometric identifier. Angular Recallo routes require particular caution around diagonal speed-line readings. |

Official Slack and Granola images and available official product favicons were inspected as visual references, kept outside the deliverable. No competitor artwork is incorporated into candidate assets. This is a limited visual similarity screen, not a trademark search or clearance opinion. A generic R and familiar punctuation remain crowded territories even where no close match was found in this sample.

## Longlist and selection

Ten mechanisms were considered: returning R leg; quotation becoming a retained baseline; stepped return path; linked double-l lettering; binding fold; waveform becoming text; clock/rewind; speech balloon/document hybrid; spiral memory; indexed paragraph mark.

The final five are the first five mechanisms. Waveforms, rewind and balloon hybrids were rejected as category shorthand. Spiral memory conflicts with the inspected Granola mark. A paragraph symbol was too close to existing editorial punctuation to justify another typographic route.

## Palette decision

This study deliberately starts with the supplied forest ink (#2C3E2D), paper (#F3F8EF), clay (#BA5A5A), moss (#8A9478) and charcoal (#171D1A). Clay is used as a large field or optional editorial accent, never required to explain a symbol. Moss is a secondary presentation swatch, not small text. Primary marks use forest on paper and paper on charcoal. A compatibility mockup also places each candidate in the current ivory/vermilion application palette; no application token is changed.

## Lettering and rights

All seven letters in every wordmark are constructed as filled vector paths in the generator. They are original lettering studies, not live-font text or a raster trace. Shared optical conventions are varied in width, contrast, terminals, R leg and double-l construction. The comparison site's interface uses the project's existing OFL-licensed DM Sans. Presentation labels in SVG use a system sans fallback; logo artwork itself has no font dependency. Candidate artwork is original project work; third-party source guidance and existing fonts retain their own licenses. Commercial/trademark clearance has not been performed.
