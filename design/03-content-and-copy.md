## Problem

## Conversations move. Details get lost.

A call brings people together. The decisions and next steps still need somewhere to land.

Recallo connects the meeting with a place to revisit its transcript and summary when insights are available.

Editorial relationship, labeled illustrative:

Conversation: “Let's move the launch review to Thursday.”

Decision: Launch review moved to Thursday.

Next step: Priya to share the revised draft before the review.

## Journey — #how-it-works

## One conversation, three connected moments.

### Before: Get ready to join.

Create a room or open an invitation. Check your camera, microphone and available devices before entering.

### During: Stay with the conversation.

Talk face to face, share your screen, and use chat when a message belongs alongside the discussion.

### After: Return to the details.

When meeting insights are ready, review the transcript, key discussion points, decisions and action items.

## Use cases

## For conversations you need to pick up again.

Project check-ins — Discuss what changed and what is blocked. Return to the decisions that guide the next working session.

Client conversations — Work through feedback together. Revisit the agreed direction before preparing the next draft.

Discovery interviews — Give the person speaking your attention. Use the available transcript to revisit details and compare interpretations afterward.

Distributed working sessions — Bring a shared problem into view. Keep the resulting decisions close for the next handover.

These are proposed uses, not customer testimonials.

## Practical questions

## Before your first conversation.

How do I join a meeting? — Choose Join a meeting and enter the room ID or paste an invitation link. You will be asked to sign in before entering the meeting.

Can I check my devices first? — The pre-join screen lets you check your camera, microphone and available devices before connecting. Your browser may ask for permission to use them.

What can I revisit afterward? — When insights are available, the meeting's insights page shows processing progress and can display a transcript and structured summary. Results are not guaranteed for every meeting.

Can I share my screen or send a message? — The meeting interface includes screen sharing and in-meeting chat. Available sharing options depend on your browser and device.
